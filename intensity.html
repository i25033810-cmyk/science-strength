Training Intensity

RPE tells you how hard a set feels. RIR means how many more good repetitions you think you could complete.

RPE reference table

RPEReps left (RIR)Simple meaning
64Easy to moderate
73Challenging
82Hard
91Very hard
100No reps left

Approximate relationship: RPE ≈ 10 − RIR. Judgement differs between lifters, especially beginners.

Estimate RPE

After a set, estimate how many more good repetitions you could do.

Estimated RPE: 8

This is a learning tool for effort regulation, not a precise measurement.

Three beginner tips

1

Learn the movement with a manageable weight before pushing intensity.

2

Use RPE as one guide alongside good technique and adequate recovery.

3

If a set feels harder than planned, consider lowering the weight or stopping the set.

Source: Helms, Cronin, Storey & Zourdos (2016), Application of the Repetitions in Reserve-Based Rating of Perceived Exertion Scale for Resistance Training. Read on PubMed.
