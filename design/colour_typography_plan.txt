SCIENCE STRENGTH — DESIGN PLAN

Purpose: A simple website introducing basic strength training to beginners.
Audience: Adults who are new to strength training.

COLOUR PALETTE
Background       #FFFFFF    clean white, easy to read
Card background  #FCFAF6    soft warm white, separates content
Main text        #22252A    strong contrast
Secondary text   #73757A    brief supporting information
Blue            #2877C8    calorie calculator
Red             #E2564D    training intensity
Amber           #D4A52A    video tutorials

TYPOGRAPHY
Headings: Arial Bold
Body: Arial Regular
Brand label: Arial Bold, all English

DESIGN CHOICES
Contrast: dark text and bright labels/buttons against a white background.
Repetition: every main function uses a matching card, round icon, and CTA button.
Alignment: titles and body text align consistently across cards.
Proximity: each description and button stays close to its function title.
Responsive: wide cards on desktop and vertically stacked cards on mobile.

INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE
index.html: Home / About / Contact
calorie.html: Calorie calculator and three goals
intensity.html: RPE and RIR guide
videos.html: Three original exercise illustrations and official JTS YouTube embeds
