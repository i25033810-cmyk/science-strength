Our idea

About Science Strength

Science Strength is a beginner-friendly website built around one idea: strength training should be easier to understand. Instead of complex features, it focuses on practical calorie estimates, a simple RPE guide, and basic exercise tutorials.

This is an educational student website, not a personal training or medical service.
