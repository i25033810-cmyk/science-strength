Daily energy (TDEE)
Estimated kcal per day—

Mifflin–St Jeor estimates resting metabolic rate (RMR), not a directly measured BMR. TDEE uses a simplified activity multiplier. Intended for adult education, not medical advice.

Three simple goals

01 · Muscle Gain

Eat a little more than your estimated daily energy needs, train regularly and include protein-rich foods.

02 · Maintenance

Start near your estimated TDEE and aim for a balanced diet and a consistent routine.

03 · Fat Loss

A small, sustainable calorie deficit may help some adults. Avoid extreme restriction and prioritise nutrition and recovery.

Activity needs vary by person. The calculation is a starting estimate, not an individualized prescription.

Sources and method

Resting metabolic rate: Mifflin MD et al. (1990), A new predictive equation for resting energy expenditure in healthy individuals. PubMed.

Physical activity level: FAO/WHO/UNU, Human energy requirements, and the U.S. NIH/NIDDK Body Weight Planner. The three multipliers (1.4 / 1.6 / 1.8) are simplified teaching choices rather than individualized official cut-offs. FAO · NIDDK.
