SCIENCE STRENGTH
ICT2215 — Assessment 2 Website Project (updated English version)

HOW TO OPEN
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge.
3. Click one of the three homepage cards to open its feature page.
4. Scroll down on the homepage to see About and Contact.

PAGES
index.html       Landing page with Home, About and Contact sections
calorie.html     RMR/TDEE educational calculator and three nutrition goals
intensity.html   RPE/RIR guide with simple calculator
videos.html      Three exercise images and JTS YouTube tutorial embeds
style.css        Shared styles and responsive layout
script.js        Small calculator functions and the demo contact form
assets/          Three original SVG exercise illustrations
design/          Homepage wireframe and colour/typography plan

CONTACT FORM
- Name and email are entered by the website VISITOR.
- Message is the visitor's sample question or feedback.
- Submit only displays confirmation. Data is NOT sent or saved.
- No creator's personal email, phone or address is needed.
